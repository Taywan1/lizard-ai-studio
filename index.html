What we do

Lizard AI Studio supports focused multilingual language tasks where clarity, format and instructions matter. From translations between different languages to concise summaries and short-form writing, every job follows the buyer’s requested language, length and structure.

A considered workflow

Technology assists.
Human judgment stays.

A structured process helps keep each delivery aligned with the request — without presenting AI output as infallible.

01
Understand the briefThe task’s language, length, format and requirements guide the work.
02
Generate a first draftAn AI model prepares a draft from the buyer’s instructions and input.
03
Check the requirementsLocal validation checks structural requirements, including JSON Schema and length limits where applicable.
04
Review before deliveryAn independent AI review checks for possible translation errors and unmet requirements. The developer then reads every result before delivery.

Accuracy with transparency

AI-generated text can contain mistakes or miss context. For important use, compare the original and translated text and request clarification when needed.

Privacy matters

Task texts are processed by third-party AI models. Please do not submit confidential, sensitive or personal information.
